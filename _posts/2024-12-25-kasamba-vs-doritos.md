The story of how a young's life was changed forever by an edible triangle on a fateful trip to Shoprite. Yamba Media presents: Kasamba vs Doritos

It all started on a regular Saturday afternoon. The fluorescent lights of Shoprite illuminated the snack aisle like a beacon of destiny. There, among the familiar packages, stood two warriors of crunch – the local champion Kasamba and the international sensation Doritos.

The decision seemed simple, but it represented so much more: tradition versus innovation, local versus global, the familiar comfort of home versus the allure of something new.

In the end, the choice was both – because at Yamba Media, we believe in celebrating diversity, honoring our roots while embracing new experiences. After all, why choose when you can enjoy the best of both worlds?

This holiday season, we're reminded that the best stories come from the simplest moments. Here's to edible triangles and the memories they create!
